Cybershot — Privacy Policy

Cybershot is developed by Title Unknown Studios. This policy covers the Cybershot application on Meta Horizon OS and explains what personal data we collect and process, why we use it, where it is retained, and how you can request its deletion. It supplements our general privacy policy and takes precedence for this app.

Request data deletion

We do not operate our own game-data servers or database. The game uses Meta’s Platform SDK and leaderboard service when online rankings are enabled. We do not use game analytics, advertising or tracking services. Receiving data through Meta’s SDK is collection and processing, including when the data is used only temporarily in the game’s memory.

Data we collect and use

When online rankings are enabled and Cybershot connects to Meta’s platform for its global leaderboard, it collects and processes User ID, User Profile and Blocked Users data as described below. Points, wave progress and a personal best are generated during play. Your best score, saved run results and preferences can remain on your headset when the online leaderboard is unavailable.

The leaderboard integration does not request your email address, real name, profile picture, friends list, location, voice or movement recordings. You do not need to create a separate Title Unknown Studios account. If you contact us, we receive the email address and information you choose to include in your message.

How data is used and shared

When online rankings are enabled, the game sends eligible scores to Meta’s leaderboard service, which associates them with your Meta account. It retrieves scores, ranks, user IDs and profile names to display leaderboard entries. Your leaderboard name, score and rank may be visible to other players. Your blocked-user list is used for filtering and is not displayed to them.

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising. Meta receives and stores leaderboard submissions and provides platform data to the game. Meta’s own handling of account and platform data is described in Meta’s privacy policy. Support and deletion emails are handled through Gmail to allow us to respond to your request.

How long data is retained

User IDs, profile names, blocked-user IDs and retrieved leaderboard entries are used in game memory and are not retained by the game after it closes. Meta retains its underlying account data separately. Online scores remain in Meta’s leaderboard until replaced by a better result or deleted. Local scores, saved run results and preferences remain until the app’s saved data is cleared. The saved local run list holds up to 20 results. Movement data is used for live gameplay and is not retained as a recording.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request and any follow-up, or to meet a legal obligation. If a legal obligation prevents deletion of particular information, we will explain what is retained and why in our response.

Request deletion of your data

You can request deletion of your Cybershot leaderboard entry and personal information held by Title Unknown Studios by emailing titleunknownstudios@gmail.com. No separate account or web form is required.

Email a deletion request

  1. Use the subject Cybershot data deletion request.
  2. Include your Meta username or display name and tell us whether you want your leaderboard entry, support correspondence, or all Cybershot personal data we hold deleted.
  3. We will reply to acknowledge the request. If necessary, we will ask for the minimum additional information needed to verify the request and identify the correct entry. A display name alone may not uniquely identify an account.
  4. We will handle verified requests without undue delay, aim to complete them within 30 days of receiving the request, and confirm the outcome by email. If verification or a legal requirement affects completion, we will explain this to you.

The button opens your email application; you must send the message to submit the request. If it does not open, copy the address into your email service. Do not send your password, authentication codes, payment details or identity documents.

Leaderboard data: We will remove the verified entry from Cybershot’s Meta-hosted leaderboard. Closing or uninstalling the game does not itself delete that online entry. Further online play can submit a new score; deletion does not permanently prevent future collection or score submissions.

Data on your headset: We cannot remotely erase local scores, saved run results or preferences. Clear Cybershot’s app data using your headset’s available storage controls, or uninstall the app to remove its local data. If a platform backup restores saved data, manage that backup through Meta’s backup controls. Contact us if you need help.

Temporary data and your Meta account: Close Cybershot to release the data held in game memory. Deleting Cybershot data does not delete your Meta account or alter its profile or blocked-user settings. Manage that separate information through your Meta account settings.

Children

Cybershot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process data from them. Meta account age requirements apply.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

Title Unknown Studios may update this policy. The effective date at the top of this page changes when we do, and the current version is always available at this URL. If we ever process data for a purpose not described here, we will update this policy before doing so.
