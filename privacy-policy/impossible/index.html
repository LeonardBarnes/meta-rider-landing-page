Impossible — Privacy Policy

Impossible is developed by Title Unknown Studios. This policy covers the Impossible application on Meta Horizon OS, and describes exactly what data the game processes, why, and how to have it removed. It supplements our general privacy policy and takes precedence for this app.

We run no servers of our own. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDK other than the Meta Platform SDK described below.

Data we process

Impossible has one online feature: a global leaderboard. It is optional — the game is fully playable with the leaderboard turned off, and nothing below is processed in that case.

DataSourceWhyWhere it goes
Meta account ID Meta Platform SDK To identify which leaderboard entry is yours Held in memory during play only
Display name Meta Platform SDK To label the other entries shown on the in-game board Held in memory during play only
Blocked-user list Meta Platform SDK So a player you have blocked never appears on your board Read into memory, used to filter, never stored
Your run result (rows crossed and elapsed time, as one number) Generated by the game as you play To rank you against other players Submitted to Meta’s leaderboard service
Your personal best Generated by the game as you play So the game can show your own best result Stored on your headset only

We do not collect your email address, real name, profile picture, friends list, location, voice, movement recordings, or any other data. We do not ask you to create an account with us, because there is no account with us to create.

How the data is processed

Your run result is submitted to Meta’s leaderboard service under the leaderboard name impossible_ascent_v1, associated with your Meta account by Meta. Meta stores it; we do not receive a copy, and we hold no database. When the in-game board is displayed, the game asks Meta for the top entries and the entries near yours, and draws them.

Your account ID, display name and blocked-user list are read from the Meta Platform SDK while the game is running, used to draw the board correctly, and discarded when the game closes. They are never written to disk, never transmitted anywhere by us, and never shared with anyone.

Your personal best is stored locally on your headset so the game can show it offline. It never leaves the device.

Sharing

We share no data with anyone. We sell no data. The only party involved is Meta, who operates the leaderboard service and whose handling of your account data is governed by Meta’s own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Your run result stays on the leaderboard until it is removed.

To have your leaderboard entry deleted, email titleunknownstudios@gmail.com from the address associated with your Meta account, or include your Meta display name. We will remove the entry within 30 days and confirm by reply.

To delete your locally stored personal best, uninstall the app from your headset.

Data held by Meta about your account is controlled by Meta, and you can manage it through your Meta account settings.

Children

Impossible is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process data from them. Meta account age requirements apply.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

Title Unknown Studios may update this policy. The effective date at the top of this page changes when we do, and the current version is always available at this URL. If we ever process data for a purpose not described here, we will update this policy before doing so.
